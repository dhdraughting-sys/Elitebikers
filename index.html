A little about us

Founded by Darren Hunt

Elite Bikers was founded in 2024 to promote Advanced and Enhanced Rider Training — helping riders learn a little, pass it on, and enjoy the ride more safely. Darren holds a F1RST pass and full membership of IAM RoadSmart, and is a qualified DVSA Enhanced Rider Instructor and Blood Biker Instructor.
